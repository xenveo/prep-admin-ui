Key points
  1. Acknowledges how the customer feelsExample: “I let her explain everything before I said anything.”
  2. Takes ownership of the problemExample: “I told him I’d stay with it until it was fixed.”
  3. Explains the concrete steps takenExample: “I checked the order history and found the delay.”
  4. States the outcome and follow-upExample: “I called back the next day to confirm it arrived.”
Red flags

  • No clear outcome
  • Promises something outside policy
